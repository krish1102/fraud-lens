# RBI FAQs on Fraud Risk Management Master Directions (April 22, 2025)

## Question 1: What triggers an account to be treated as a Red Flagged Account (RFA)?
**Answer**: An account is treated as an RFA when suspicion of fraudulent activity arises based on the occurrence of one or more Early Warning Signals (EWS). These signals include abnormal velocity spikes, unusual deviations in transaction amounts compared to historical customer baselines, transactions routed through high-risk device/IP hubs, or non-cooperation during customer contact attempts.

## Question 2: Can automated machine learning or rule-based models classify an account as fraud autonomously?
**Answer**: No. Automated models, AI algorithms, and anomaly detection systems serve as early alert generators and investigative decision-support tools. A formal declaration or classification of fraud requires human analyst review, validation of evidence, adherence to due process, and approval from the designated authority / Fraud Monitoring Committee.

## Question 3: How should institutions maintain auditability of investigation decisions?
**Answer**: Regulated entities must maintain a digital, auditable case management trail for each flagged transaction and investigation case. This includes logging the model versions, specific contributing risk indicators, analyst notes, communication timestamps, and final disposition rationale.
