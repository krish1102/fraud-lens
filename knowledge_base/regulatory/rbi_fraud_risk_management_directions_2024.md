# RBI Master Directions on Fraud Risk Management in Commercial Banks & Financial Institutions (July 15, 2024)

## 1. Regulatory Context & Objectives
The Reserve Bank of India issued comprehensive revised Master Directions on Fraud Risk Management on July 15, 2024, to strengthen the governance and risk-management architecture across commercial banks, regional rural banks, and financial institutions.

## 2. Core Governance & Oversight Mandates
- **Board Oversight**: The Board of Directors shall possess overall responsibility for fraud risk management. Banks must establish a Special Committee of the Board for Monitoring and Review of Frauds (SCBF).
- **Fraud Risk Management Framework**: Regulated entities (REs) must institute a robust Fraud Risk Management Policy approved by their Boards, periodically reviewed at least once every three years.
- **Dedicated Fraud Monitoring Unit (FMU)**: REs must establish a dedicated Special Fraud Monitoring Cell / FMU equipped with appropriate data analytics and transaction monitoring tools.

## 3. Early Warning Signals (EWS) & Red Flagging
- **Requirement for Analytical Systems**: Regulated entities must deploy automated data analytics, transaction pattern recognition, and machine-learning models to capture Early Warning Signals (EWS) in real time or near-real time.
- **Red Flagging Accounts (RFA)**: Any transaction or customer profile exhibiting unexplained velocity surges, mule account characteristics, shared device fingerprints, or deviation from registered business profiles must be tagged as a Red Flagged Account (RFA).
- **Time-bound Scrutiny**: Once an account is marked with Red Flag indicators, a preliminary investigation must commence immediately, and a decision on fraud classification must be taken within prescribed regulatory windows (not exceeding 6 months for credit/operational frauds).

## 4. Reporting & Information Sharing
- Frauds must be reported to the RBI through the Central Fraud Registry (CFR) and to Central / State Law Enforcement Agencies (such as the Central Bureau of Investigation or state cyber crime police) based on financial thresholds.
