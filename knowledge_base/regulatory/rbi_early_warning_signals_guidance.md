# RBI Guidance on Early Warning Signals (EWS) and Market Intelligence

## 1. Executive Summary
On July 15, 2024, the Reserve Bank of India announced revisions to the Master Directions on Fraud Risk Management, emphasizing the adoption of modern data analytics, artificial intelligence, and proactive market intelligence systems to identify and mitigate fraud before financial losses crystallize.

## 2. Mandatory Technological Integration
- **Transaction-Level Behavioral Analytics**: Regulated institutions are directed to implement statistical and machine-learning baselines for customer behaviors. Deviations in transaction amount, velocity, channel, and geographic origin must automatically trigger early alert scores.
- **Entity & Network Graphs**: Institutions should utilize linkage analytics across transaction metadata (e.g. shared device IDs, IP addresses, virtual payment addresses, and beneficiary accounts) to identify coordinated syndicates and mule chains.
- **Explainability Requirement**: Analytical alerting mechanisms must provide transparent indicators or contributing factors so fraud review analysts can understand why an alert was triggered and execute informed triage.

## 3. Principles of Natural Justice
- The revised directions emphasize adherence to principles of natural justice before classifying accounts as fraud, mandating an opportunity for the borrower or customer to explain anomalies, supported by an auditable record of investigative findings.
